VLDB 2026 Satellite Event: Conference Venue

The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

No. 1 Duxue Road, Dongchong Town
Nansha District, Guangzhou
Guangdong Province, China

VLDB 2026 Satellite Event will be held at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), HKUST(GZ). The campus is located within the Qingsheng Hub Block in Nansha District, at the geographical center of the Greater Bay Area.

The university is adjacent to Qingsheng High-Speed Railway Station and Metro Line 4, connecting the campus with downtown Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Hong Kong, and other cities in the Greater Bay Area.

Meeting Venues: Activity Center

The Activity Center provides two adjacent event spaces: C2-103, with a capacity of 300 people in an open-format configuration, and C2-102, with a capacity of 150 people in a seated configuration.

Public Transport

  • Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN): approximately 1.5–2 hours by airport bus and metro, 2 hours by metro, or 1.5 hours by taxi / ride-hailing.
  • Guangzhou South Railway Station: approximately 1 hour by metro, or 40–50 minutes by taxi / ride-hailing.
