VLDB 2026 Satellite Event: Transportation Guide

How do I get to VLDB 2026 Satellite Event?

Conference Venue: The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

Address: No. 1 Duxue Road, Dongchong Town, Nansha District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China
For your driver: 香港科技大学（广州），广州市南沙区东涌镇笃学路1号

The campus can be reached from Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN), Hong Kong International Airport (HKG), or Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX). The nearby metro stop is Dongchong Station (东涌), Line 4, Exit A.

Station names: When booking high-speed rail tickets, search for Nanshabei / Nansha North (南沙北), formerly Qingsheng Railway Station. Its connecting Line 4 metro station is Qingsheng (庆盛 / 南沙北站).

Journey times below are approximate planning estimates. Check the operators' latest routes, timetables, fares, and terminal information before travelling.

Option 1: Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport (CAN)

1.1 By Metro

  1. Follow signs for Guangzhou Metro at your arrival terminal and take Line 3 towards the city.
  2. Continue on Line 3 to Haibang, changing at Tiyu Xilu if required by the train service.
  3. At Haibang, change to Line 4 towards Nansha Passenger Port and alight at Dongchong.
  4. Use Exit A and follow the local signs to HKUST(GZ).

Allow approximately 2 hours. Plan your journey using the Guangzhou Metro official website.

1.2 By Taxi / Ride-hailing

Use the airport's designated taxi pick-up area or the pick-up point shown in your ride-hailing app. Set the destination to HKUST(GZ), not the university's Hong Kong campus. Allow approximately 1.5 hours, depending on traffic; confirm the fare with the operator.

1.3 By Airport Bus and Metro

Check for an airport coach to Nansha Jiaomen Bus Station. From Jiaomen, take Line 4 towards Huangcun to Dongchong, then use Exit A for the campus. Allow approximately 1.5–2 hours, subject to coach availability and waiting time. Confirm the current boarding location and service with the airport before departure.

Option 2: Hong Kong International Airport (HKG)

2.1 By Airport Express and High-speed Rail

  1. Take the Airport Express to Kowloon Station, then follow the pedestrian signs to Hong Kong West Kowloon high-speed railway station. A taxi to West Kowloon is another option.
  2. Book a train that stops at Nanshabei (南沙北). Check the actual stopping pattern before purchasing; alternatively, travel to Guangzhou South and use Option 4 below.
  3. At Nanshabei, follow signs to Qingsheng Metro Station. Take Line 4 towards Huangcun for one stop to Dongchong, then use Exit A for the campus.

See the Airport Express information and MTR High Speed Rail destinations and booking information. Allow additional time for transfers and station procedures.

Option 3: Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport (SZX)

3.1 By Metro and High-speed Rail

  1. Take Shenzhen Metro Line 11 from Airport Station to Qianhaiwan.
  2. Change to Line 5 and alight at Shenzhen North (Shenzhenbei) Railway Station.
  3. Take a high-speed train stopping at Nanshabei (南沙北). Check train availability and stops on China Railway 12306.
  4. Transfer to Guangzhou Metro Line 4 at Qingsheng, travel one stop towards Huangcun to Dongchong, then use Exit A for the campus.

Check the airport's official metro connections for current operating information.

3.2 By Taxi / Ride-hailing

A direct taxi or ride-hailing journey to HKUST(GZ) avoids rail transfers. Confirm the destination, estimated journey time, fare, and any tolls with the operator.

Option 4: Guangzhou South Railway Station

4.1 By Metro

  1. Take Line 7 to Higher Education Mega Center South (大学城南).
  2. Change to Line 4 towards Nansha Passenger Port and alight at Dongchong.
  3. Use Exit A and follow signs to HKUST(GZ).

Allow approximately 1 hour.

4.2 By Taxi / Ride-hailing

Set your destination to HKUST(GZ), No. 1 Duxue Road, Nansha District. Allow approximately 40–50 minutes, depending on traffic.
